About SaloneHealth

Bridging the Health Gap in Sierra Leone

Sierra Leone faces significant health challenges — from limited facility access in rural areas to disease outbreaks. SaloneHealth connects citizens to verified health facilities, real-time alerts, and government health resources.

  • All 16 districts mapped with verified facilities
  • Real-time disease outbreak notifications
  • Free registration for all Sierra Leonean citizens
  • Partnered with Ministry of Health & Sanitation
  • Mobile-first design for low-bandwidth access

Our Story

Why We Started

Founded in 2020, SaloneHealth was created to address the critical information gap in Sierra Leone's healthcare system. Many citizens struggle to find reliable health facilities, access emergency services, or receive timely disease alerts.

We partnered with the Ministry of Health & Sanitation to create a digital platform that makes healthcare more accessible, transparent, and responsive to the needs of all Sierra Leoneans.

Our Vision

Healthier Sierra Leone

We envision a Sierra Leone where every citizen has easy access to reliable health information, verified medical facilities, and timely emergency response — regardless of where they live.

Through technology and partnership, we're building the digital health infrastructure that Sierra Leone deserves.
