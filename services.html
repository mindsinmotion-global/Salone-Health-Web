Service Details

How Our Services Work

Maternal & Child Health Programs

Our maternal health initiatives focus on reducing maternal and child mortality across Sierra Leone. We partner with district health teams to provide:

  • Antenatal care and nutritional support
  • Supervised delivery services at health facilities
  • Postnatal follow-up for mother and baby
  • Childhood vaccination schedules
  • Newborn screening and early intervention

Disease Testing & Lab Services

We provide rapid diagnostic testing for priority diseases affecting Sierra Leone:

  • Malaria rapid diagnostic tests (RDTs)
  • HIV/AIDS testing and counselling
  • Tuberculosis sputum smear microscopy
  • Cholera rapid tests during outbreaks
  • Pregnancy tests at community level

Emergency & 24/7 Response

Our emergency services ensure rapid response to health crises:

  • Emergency hotline coordination
  • Ambulance dispatch services
  • First-responder training
  • Emergency facility referral
  • 24-hour availability
